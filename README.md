## 📌 Project Overview

This project delivers a **comprehensive Sales Analytics Dashboard** built entirely in **Microsoft Excel**. It transforms raw sales data into powerful business insights through dynamic pivot tables, interactive charts, and a professional executive dashboard — enabling data-driven decision-making for sales teams and management.

> 🎯 **Goal**: Provide a 360° view of sales performance across products, regions, time periods, and sales representatives — all in one interactive Excel workbook.

## 📊 Dashboard Sections

### 1️⃣ Executive KPI Summary
- Total Revenue, Units Sold, Average Order Value
- Month-over-Month (MoM) and Year-over-Year (YoY) growth
- Profit Margin and Target Achievement Rate

### 2️⃣ Sales Trend Analysis
- Monthly revenue trend line chart
- Seasonal patterns and peak periods identification
- Quarterly performance comparison

### 3️⃣ Product Performance
- Top-selling products by revenue and volume
- Product category contribution breakdown
- Slow-moving inventory identification

### 4️⃣ Regional Sales Analysis
- Geographic breakdown of sales performance
- Region-wise revenue and growth comparison
- Territory performance ranking

### 5️⃣ Sales Representative Analysis
- Individual rep performance metrics
- Target vs. Actual achievement tracking
- Commission and bonus calculation tracker

### 6️⃣ Customer Analysis
- Top customers by revenue contribution
- Customer segmentation and RFM insights
- Repeat vs. new customer ratio

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

| Metric | Insight |
|--------|---------|
| 🏆 Best Month | Highest sales typically in Q4 (Oct–Dec) |
| 📦 Top Category | Electronics/High-margin products drive majority revenue |
| 🌍 Best Region | Metro regions consistently outperform rural |
| 👤 Star Performer | Top 20% reps generate 80% of revenue (Pareto Principle) |
| 📉 Weakness | Q1 sees lowest sales — opportunity for promotional campaigns |

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Download** `dhyey_sales_projcet_b8.xlsx`
2. **Open** in Microsoft Excel (2016 or later)
3. Navigate to the **📊 Dashboard** tab for the executive overview
4. Use **Slicers** to filter by Region, Product Category, or Time Period
5. Use the **Timeline** control to select specific date ranges
6. Drill into individual sheets for detailed analysis

## 📋 Data Requirements

To use this template with your own data, prepare a CSV/sheet with:
- `Date`, `Region`, `Salesperson`, `Product`, `Category`
- `Units Sold`, `Unit Price`, `Revenue`, `Cost`, `Profit`
- `Customer Name`, `Customer Segment`, `Order ID`
